# Does Medicare pay for a home safety assessment?

*By Russ L'HommeDieu, DPT, ATP | Home Safety Solutions*

Usually not, at least not as a stand-alone service. Original Medicare does not list an independent home safety assessment as a covered benefit, and it does not pay for grab bars, ramps or stair lifts. Medicare may cover some home safety work when it is part of a home health or therapy plan of care. Some Medicare Advantage plans and other programs help with devices and changes.

## When Medicare may cover part of it

| Pathway | What it includes | The catch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medicare home health | PT and OT visits at home, which often include home safety recommendations | You must be homebound, need skilled care, and have a doctor's order and in-person visit. The agency must be Medicare-certified. |
| Outpatient PT or OT | Therapy for balance, strength and daily tasks under a plan of care | Usually delivered in a clinic, with 20 percent coinsurance after the Part B deductible. |
| Annual Wellness Visit | A review of fall risk and home safety, by observation or screening questions | It is a checklist in the doctor's office, not a visit to your home. |

## What Original Medicare does not cover

Medicare's national equipment list denies grab bars as self-help devices that are not primarily medical. It denies elevators and stairway elevators as convenience items. Bath seats and bathtub lifts are also denied.

## Other programs that may help

- **Medicare Advantage.** Since 2019, plans may offer home and bathroom safety devices and modifications as supplemental benefits. Few do. A federal GAO report found that about 12.5 percent of plans offered home and bathroom safety benefits. Only 1.4 percent offered structural home modifications for chronically ill members in 2022. Call your plan and ask.
- **Florida Medicaid long-term care.** The Statewide Medicaid Managed Care Long-Term Care program can include home accessibility adaptations such as grab bars. Eligibility requires Medicaid and a nursing-home level of care, and there is a waitlist. Call the Florida Elder Helpline at 1-800-963-5337.
- **VA HISA grants.** Veterans may qualify for Home Improvements and Structural Alterations grants. The lifetime limit is $6,800 for service-connected conditions, or for veterans rated 50 percent or more service-connected. It is $2,000 for other eligible veterans.
- **Taxes, HSAs and FSAs.** IRS Publication 502 lists ramps, wider doorways, railings, grab bars and bathroom changes made for a medical reason as possible medical expenses. Ask your tax adviser or plan administrator how this applies to you.
