Home Video Walkthrough Guide

For family members preparing for a Ready for Discharge video session. Getting these things ready takes about 20 minutes and makes the session faster and more accurate.

Before the call

  • Charge your phone or tablet fully and bring the charger.
  • Have a tape measure, a pen, and this sheet.
  • Turn on every light, and open blinds in the rooms we will visit.
  • Clear a path so you can walk slowly with the camera.
  • Test your video link a few minutes early from inside the home.

Measurements we will ask for

WhereWhat to measureYour note
EntryNumber of steps, height of each step, and whether there is a rail on each side
DoorwaysClear width of the entry, bedroom, and bathroom doors when open
ThresholdsHeight of any raised threshold, including the slider to the lanai
Step-downsHeight of any step into a sunken room or the garage
BedFloor to the top of the mattress; space on the side they get out of
ToiletFloor to the top of the seat; space on each side
Tub or showerHeight of the tub wall or shower curb; any grab bars and where
ChairSeat height of the chair they use most

The route we will follow

  1. Outside the front door, looking at the steps and the path from the driveway
  2. The path from the entry to the bedroom, at walking pace
  3. The bedroom and both sides of the bed
  4. The bathroom they will use, including the tub or shower and toilet
  5. The kitchen and where meals will happen
  6. Where equipment will go: walker, wheelchair, commode, oxygen
  7. Any lanai, pool deck, or garage entry they use

Filming tips

  • Hold the phone sideways, and move slowly.
  • Point the camera at the floor at every change: rugs, thresholds, steps.
  • Put the tape measure in the picture when you read a measurement.
  • If you can, have a second person hold the phone while you measure.

Sessions are not recorded. Any screenshots we take are stored only in the secure client portal.
