For families caring for someone they love

Whether you live down the street or in New York, you should not have to guess whether Mom or Dad is safe at home.

A hospital stay just happened

Book Ready for Discharge while your parent is still in the facility. You join by video from the home, or from wherever you are, and get a report within 24 hours.

You noticed a change

A slower walk, a bruise nobody explains, a shower that is not happening. A Home Safety Visit gives you a clinical picture and a plan.

In short

Home Safety Solutions helps families who are caring for a parent or loved one, whether they live nearby or out of state. Dr. Russ L'HommeDieu, DPT, ATP, assesses the person, their equipment and the home, and family can join by video. After a hospital stay, book Ready for Discharge; otherwise a Home Safety Visit. Both are $250 in the home around Tampa Bay or $175 by video in Florida, New York and Ohio, and both start with a $50 phone consultation.

Caring from a distance

Many of our families live out of state. You can join any assessment by video, receive the report through our secure portal, and speak with Dr. L'HommeDieu directly afterward. If the home is out of state, start with a core assessment by video with a Remote Caregiving deep dive. Your parent stays in charge: they sign a Release Authorization naming you, and we share everything you need.

What if my parent does not want this?

That is common, and it is worth taking seriously. People accept help more readily when it is framed as staying independent, not as losing it. A few things that tend to work:

  • Present the visit as a way to stay in the home they love.
  • Let them book the Initial Consultation and talk with Dr. L'HommeDieu first.
  • Promise that nothing gets changed without their say.

If you are the legal decision maker

If you hold health care power of attorney or are a court-appointed guardian, you can book and sign on your parent's behalf. Bring or upload the document, and we will note your authority on the forms.
