Start here: book your $50 Initial Consultation

The Initial Consultation is a $50 conversation with Dr. L'HommeDieu that helps you decide what, if anything, you need next. It is the first step for every service.

What it is

  • A 30 minute phone call with Russ L'HommeDieu, DPT, ATP
  • A conversation about the person, the home, the people who help, and what worries you
  • A clear recommendation for the right next step, or an honest answer that you do not need one
  • Open to the person themselves, family members, and caregivers; more than one person can join
  • $50, credited toward any visit you book within 30 days
  • No obligation to book anything else

What it is not

  • Not a home assessment. We do not see the home, so there is no photo report, hazard list, or readiness rating. Those come with a visit.
  • Not a medical visit. There is no examination, diagnosis, treatment, or physical therapy, and it does not replace your doctor or therapist.
  • Not billed to insurance. It is a private-pay service and is not billed to Medicare or other insurance.
  • Not for urgent discharges. If someone is in the hospital or rehab now, book Ready for Discharge below instead.
  • Not for professionals referring patients. Physicians, therapists, discharge planners, and facilities can book a free partner call below.
  • Not for emergencies. If someone has fallen and is hurt, call 911.
