Why the home often gets missed

Every discharge plan assumes a home that can support it. Most of the time, nobody has looked.

Discharge plans are written for the patient, not the house

Case managers work under time pressure without training in home environments. Home health therapy starts after the person is already home. Contractors can build, but they do not assess how a person moves.

Falls are where the gap shows up first

About one in four adults age 65 and older falls each year, and falls lead to roughly one million hospital stays a year. Many start with a step, a threshold, or a trip to the bathroom in the dark.

Florida homes bring their own hazards

Sunken living rooms, pool decks, slider tracks, and garage entry steps. Then hurricane season, when a power outage becomes a medical event for anyone on oxygen or a powered bed.

Fall figures: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, Facts About Falls, 2026.

How it works

  1. Talk with us

    A 30 minute phone consultation about the person, the home, and your goals. $50, credited toward any visit you book within 30 days.

  2. We assess the person and the home

    In person, or by guided video before a hospital discharge. We look at how the person moves, not just at the house.

  3. You get a written plan

    A photo report, a prioritized hazard list, and recommendations, delivered to a secure portal, followed by a call to walk through it.

  4. Changes get made right

    We write exact specifications for the contractor you choose, then verify the finished work before anyone relies on it.

What you receive after a Home Safety Visit

  • A photo report of every room we assessed
  • A prioritized hazard list: fix now, within 30 days, and planned
  • Fall risk screening using the CDC STEADI approach

Nothing about you, without you

Your home and your choices stay yours. We hold a strict “Nothing About Me…Without Me” policy: nothing gets changed, recommended, or set in motion without your say.

We assess, explain the options, and wait for your decision. You are always the one who decides what happens next.

What this means for you

  • No change is made to your home without your consent
  • Every recommendation is explained before you decide
  • You choose which items to act on, and when
  • You can pause, decline, or change your mind at any point
  • Your priorities lead the plan, start to finish
