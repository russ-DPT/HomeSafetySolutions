Clinician-led assessment vs a contractor’s free check

In short

A contractor’s free check looks at the house, and it is usually done by the company that would be paid to do the work. A clinician-led assessment from Home Safety Solutions looks at the person, their equipment and the home together, is done by Dr. Russ L'HommeDieu, DPT, ATP, and has no tie to who does the work. It costs $250 in the home or $175 by video.

Ready for Discharge vs Home Safety Visit

In short

Choose Ready for Discharge when someone is coming home from a hospital or rehab stay and you need to know whether the home can support them on day one. Choose the Home Safety Visit when the person is already home and you want to lower their fall risk. Both are $250 in the home or $175 by video.
