Right Home Assessment

Will this home work, now and in ten years?

Before you buy, rent or move, find out whether the house fits the person who will live there, and what it would take to make it work.

$250 in your homeIn person only

Is this for you?

  • You are buying or renting a home and want to know it will work long term.
  • You are downsizing, or moving to a single-level home.
  • You are moving a parent closer to family.
  • You are deciding whether to stay in the home you have, or move.

What happens

Dr. L'HommeDieu walks the home with you, in person, and looks at it through the needs of the person who will live there, today and as needs change. We look at:

  • Entry and steps
  • The bathroom
  • Where the bedroom is
  • Kitchen reach
  • Doorways and hallways
  • Flooring
  • Lighting
  • Storm and power exposure
  • Distance to help and services

What you get

  • A fit rating for the person, now and in ten years
  • A photo report of the barriers we found
  • A list of the changes that would be needed, with priorities

Add a deep dive

Your report already touches on each of these. A deep dive goes further, with its own written plan. Dr. L'HommeDieu can add one on the consultation call.

Common questions

Can you assess a home I have not bought yet?

Yes. We can assess a home you are thinking of buying or renting, as long as you can arrange access with the owner or agent. Many families book it before making an offer.

Can you do it by video?

No. The Right Home Assessment is only offered in person, inside our in-person service area, because the decision depends on details we need to measure and see.

Will you tell me what the changes cost?

We specify exactly what changes are needed. Contractors quote them, and you choose who does the work. We take no payments from contractors.
