Is this for you?

  • You have had a Home Safety Visit and want the home re-checked as needs change.
  • Someone in the home depends on power for medical equipment and needs a plan every season.
  • Family lives at a distance and wants regular check-ins.
  • You want to be first in line when something changes.

What happens

Membership is set up with you on the $50 consultation call. From there, we schedule the year with you: one in-home visit with your full written report, and three short telehealth check-ins between visits, plus priority scheduling.

Monthly memberships require a 12-month commitment. Annual memberships are paid once per year. See membership terms.
