How our prices work

  1. Start with the $50 Initial Consultation

  2. One trip, one travel fee

    Add a second service to the same visit and pay only the add-on price.

  3. Virtual visits cost less

    Virtual visits cost less than in-home visits because there is no drive and no home visit.

    Paperwork prepared for a contractor is a separate service, except where noted.

Payment

How payment works

For now, we take card payments by phone: after you book, we call you to confirm the appointment and take payment. Travel fees and add-ons are taken on the same call. You pay any contractor directly; we never collect money for construction.

About Medicare

Our services are private-pay consultative services and are not billed to Medicare or other insurance. If you have Medicare, we will give you a written notice explaining this before your first service.
