No listing, vetted or not, is a guarantee of any contractor's work. Always sign a written contract, confirm permits, and review the finished work. A Build Verify Visit checks it for you.

How we vet every partner

  1. Paper screen

    Florida DBPR license lookup, liability and workers' compensation certificates, CAPS status, reviews, and permit history.

  2. Interview

    Working to a clinician's written specification, response time after discharge, and itemized pricing.

  3. References

    Two past aging-in-place clients and one supplier.

  4. Paid trial job

    A small first job, with our clinician on site at installation.

  5. Ongoing scorecard

    Every job is rated on following the spec, timeliness, fair pricing, communication, cleanliness, and verification. Two below-standard jobs in a row means removal.

Florida rules we follow

  • Handymen may do small jobs only under Florida's exemption: under $2,500 in labor and materials, no permit, and outside licensed trades.
  • Any electrical work, even replacing an outlet or light fixture, goes to a licensed electrician.
  • Ramps, roll-in showers, and door widening need a licensed contractor and permits.
