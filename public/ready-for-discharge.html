Is this for you?

  • A family member is in the hospital or rehab and will be discharged soon.
  • You are not sure the home can support them on day one.
  • You live out of the area and need to know what to set up before they arrive.
  • You are a discharge planner who wants the home checked before the patient leaves.

What happens

  1. The $50 consultation

    A family member or discharge planner books the call and gives the expected discharge date. Dr. L'HommeDieu schedules the discharge visit with you on that call.

  2. Get the home ready

    For a video visit, the family receives our Home Video Walkthrough Guide: a tape measure, good light and a charged phone are most of it. Forms are handled in our secure client portal.

  3. The visit

    About an hour. Dr. L'HommeDieu talks with the patient about their daily routine and concerns, then walks the home with the family: entry, the path to the bedroom, bathroom, kitchen and where equipment will go.

  4. Report within 24 hours

    The Discharge Home Readiness Report is posted to the portal, followed by a call to go over every Red and Yellow item.

  5. Seven-day check-in

    A short call after the patient is home: were the must-do items done, any falls or ER visits, anything new.

What you get

  • A readiness rating across ten areas: getting in, moving through, bed and transfers, bathroom, kitchen and meals, equipment, people and caregiver coverage, supplies and routines, safety and emergencies, and follow-up
  • A written report within 24 hours
  • A call to go over every Red and Yellow item
  • A check-in call seven days after discharge

Common questions

Can it be done while my family member is still in the hospital?

Yes. We assess the home, not the hospital care. A family member can meet us at the home or walk us through it by video. With written permission, we can send the report to the discharge planner or case manager.

What if discharge is only a few days away?

Book the earliest consultation time you see. Dr. L'HommeDieu schedules the discharge visit on that call, and rush scheduling within 48 hours is available for $50.

Is the rating a medical clearance?

No. The rating describes whether the home can support this person. Medical decisions about discharge stay with the hospital team.
