Home / For facilities

For hospitals, rehab, and skilled nursing facilities

A fast, credible check of the home before discharge, so your patients do not bounce back.

What your team gets

  • Ready for Discharge sessions scheduled around your discharge date, with a rush option within 24 hours
  • A Discharge Home Readiness Report within 24 hours of the session
  • A same-day call when a home is rated Red, with patient authorization
  • A 7-day check-in with the family on must-do items, falls, and ER visits
  • In-home follow-up within 72 hours of a discharge notice when video is not enough

A 20-case pilot

Start with a defined pilot. We track completion of must-do items by arrival day, falls, ER visits, and readmissions reported at 30 and 90 days, and share the results with your team.

Privacy

We treat all protected health information under HIPAA. Clinical records and reports live in a secure portal, and we share reports with your team only with the patient's signed authorization.
