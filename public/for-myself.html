Home / For myself

A safer home, on your terms

You know your home better than anyone. We help you see it the way a physical therapist would, so you can keep living there with confidence.

In short

Home Safety Solutions helps adults who want to keep living safely in their own home. Dr. Russ L'HommeDieu, DPT, ATP, looks at you, your equipment and your home together, and you decide what changes. Start with a $50, 30-minute phone consultation; a Home Safety Visit is $250 in your home around Tampa Bay, or $175 by video in Florida, New York and Ohio. See all services and prices.

Is this for you?

  • You have had a fall, a near fall, or you have started holding on to furniture.
  • You are coming home from the hospital or rehab soon.
  • Stairs, the tub, or getting up from a chair takes more effort than it used to.
  • You depend on oxygen, a powered bed, or medicine that needs refrigeration.
  • You are thinking about buying or moving to a new home.

Where to start

Start with a $50 Initial Consultation: a 30-minute phone call with Dr. L'HommeDieu. He listens to what is going on, and recommends the right service for you. That is often a Home Safety Visit, where he watches how you get in and out of the house, the bed, the chair, the shower and the car, and gives you a photo report and a prioritized list of what to change.

Coming home from the hospital? See Ready for Discharge. Depending on oxygen or a powered bed? Add a Storm Ready Audit, a $75 deep dive added to a core assessment. Thinking about a move? See the Right Home Assessment.

You decide what to change. Nothing gets done without your say.

Want someone to hear the results with you?

Many people like a son, daughter, or friend to see the report. You can name them on our Release Authorization, which you sign in our secure client portal after booking, and we will share it with them. You can change your mind at any time.
