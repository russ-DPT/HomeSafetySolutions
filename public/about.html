A clinician, not a salesperson, in your home

Home Safety Solutions exists to close one gap: the space between a discharge plan and the home that has to carry it out.

Russ L'HommeDieu, DPT, ATP

Dr. L'HommeDieu has practiced physical therapy for more than 35 years, including long service as a VA Home Safety Coordinator on a home-based primary care team. He holds the Assistive Technology Professional credential from Rehabilitation Engineering and Assistive Technology Society of North America (RESNA) and is trained in aging-in-place design and modification through the National Association of Home Builders. He teaches in Doctor of Physical Therapy programs.

He earned his Doctor of Physical Therapy at Touro College and is a doctoral candidate in health education and leadership.

The person first

We assess how someone actually moves, thinks, and gets through the day, then judge the home against that. A hazard list without the person is only half the answer.

Independent by design

We prescribe; licensed contractors build; you hire them directly. We accept no referral fees, so our recommendations answer only to you.

Measured results

We follow up at 7 days and track what gets done, falls, and ER visits, so we can prove what works and improve what does not.

Our framework

Every assessment follows the Systems of Systems approach Dr. L'HommeDieu developed and teaches. It brings together three established lenses: the immediate physical and social environment of the home, the social determinants of health, and the World Health Organization's International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health. In plain terms: the room, the life around it, and the person in it.
