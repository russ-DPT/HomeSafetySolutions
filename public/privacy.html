Privacy

Plain language about what this website collects, and what it does not.

This website does not collect medical information

We do not ask for diagnoses, medicines, or health history on this website, in online booking, or by email. Clinical information is collected only in our secure client portal, which operates under a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement.

Booking

Online booking, online forms, and our secure client portal are provided by PracticeQ (IntakeQ). When you book, PracticeQ collects the contact details the booking form asks for, such as your name, email, and phone number. Please do not enter health information in booking forms.

Payments

When you book online, you enter your card directly into our booking and payment system, IntakeQ, which stores it with its card processor. We charge it after your consultation, or sooner only if our cancellation or no-show policy applies. Payments you make by phone are entered directly into the same system during the call. We do not write down, email, or keep your full card number.

Contractor applications

Information contractors submit is used only to evaluate and manage their participation in the network.

Your settings

If you change the text size, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. The fall risk quick check is scored in your browser and never sent to us.

Protected health information

We treat all protected health information under HIPAA. We share reports only with people you name on a signed Release Authorization. You may revoke an authorization at any time in writing.

Contact

Questions about privacy: (813) 867-3372 or info@homesafety.solutions.
