Services and pricing

Every home is different, but our pricing is simple. Start with a phone call. On the call, we choose the right core assessment together and add a deep dive only if you need one.

  1. 1

    Talk with us

    $50

    A 30-minute call with Dr. L'HommeDieu. Useful on its own, and credited in full if you book.

  2. 2

    One core assessment

    $250

    In your home, or $175 by video where offered. Your $50 is credited toward it.

  3. 3

    Add a deep dive, if needed

    +$75 or $150

    Optional. Goes further on one topic your report already touches.

The call is worth it, even if you stop there

There is no obligation to book anything else. You leave the call with real answers.

On the $50 call

  • A physical therapist listens to your concerns about the person and the home
  • Answers to your questions and practical advice, as time allows
  • An honest recommendation on what to do next, including when you do not need a visit

What a core assessment adds

  • Dr. L'HommeDieu sees the person move through the actual home
  • A full assessment and a comprehensive written analysis
  • A prioritized plan you can act on
