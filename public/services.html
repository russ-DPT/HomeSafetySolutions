Ready for Discharge: Virtual

A guided video assessment before the patient leaves the hospital or rehab. We interview the patient, walk the home by video with a family member, and post a Discharge Home Readiness Report within 24 hours, with a 7-day check-in call. Includes a check that the walker, commode, wheelchair, or other equipment going home will fit and work in the house.

Add a Remote Caregiving Plan to have alerts and safe entry in place before the first night home.

Home Safety Visit

A clinical assessment of the person, the home, and the support system, including fall risk screening. You receive a photo report, a prioritized hazard list, and recommendations within 48 hours, followed by a debrief call. Available in your home or by video.
