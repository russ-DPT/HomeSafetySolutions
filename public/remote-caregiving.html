Remote Caregiving Plan

Caring for someone from far away

When you can't be there every day, the right technology can help you know they are safe. Most families buy devices one at a time and end up with apps that don't work together and alerts no one trusts. We design the whole system first, a qualified installer puts it in, and we train your family to use it.

Every core assessment report touches on this topic. The deep dive goes further, with its own written plan. Dr. L'HommeDieu can add it to your visit on the consultation call.

In your core reportWhere a fall could go unseen, and how help would get in.

In the deep diveA room-by-room technology plan: fall detection, safe entry, video check-ins, lighting and stove safety, with family training by video.

Is this for you?

  • You live out of the area or out of state from someone you care for.
  • An older adult lives alone and wants to stay in their own home.
  • The family is adjusting after a hospital stay, a fall or a new diagnosis.
  • You have bought devices that are not working together.

What happens

  1. The $50 consultation

    We talk through goals, concerns and who is involved in care, and schedule a core assessment with the Remote Caregiving deep dive added.

  2. Assessment

    During your core assessment, in the home or by video, we look at the person, their daily routines, the home layout, Wi-Fi and cell signal, devices already in place, and each family member's role.

  3. Your written plan

    A room-by-room plan with devices, specifications, placement and priorities.

  4. Installation

    You choose a qualified installer and pay them directly. You share your written plan with them. The plan does not include a Contractor Consultation. If you want us to prepare a contractor-ready prescription and talk with your installer, add a Contractor Consultation for $150.

  5. Family training

    A video session to set up alerts and practice what each person does when an alert comes in, then a follow-up call after installation.

What the plan covers

  • Fall detection: wearable and camera-free options, matched to how the person lives
  • Safe entry: smart door locks with separate, scheduled codes for aides, family and emergency responders
  • The front door: video doorbells so the person and family can see who is there
  • Staying in touch: a video calling setup simple enough to use every day
  • Lighting: automatic lighting from bed to bathroom and at entrances
  • Environmental controls: voice or app control matched to hand function, hearing, vision and memory
  • Kitchen safety: automatic stove shutoff options for electric and gas ranges
  • Heat and cooling: thermostat alerts when the home gets too hot or too cold
  • Storms and outages: what still works without power or internet, and a simple low-tech plan

What you get

  • A written Remote Caregiving Plan: room-by-room devices, specifications, placement and priorities
  • An Alert and Response Plan: who receives each alert and what they do
  • A Storm and Outage Plan
  • A Home Monitoring Agreement, reviewed with the person being monitored or their legal representative
  • One family training session by video (up to 60 minutes, any number of family members) and a follow-up call after installation

Qualified installers

Many plug-in devices can be set up by family, with our guidance by video. Hardwired lighting, door locks, stove shutoffs, thermostats and backup power go to qualified installers, such as licensed electricians, security and low-voltage installers, and HVAC contractors. Any work on a gas range goes to a licensed gas contractor. You choose your installer, contract with them and pay them directly. A Contractor Consultation is not part of the plan; it can be added for $150. See our Contractor Network.

Privacy and respect

Monitoring should protect independence, not take it away. We plan with the person being monitored, not just about them. We do not recommend cameras in bedrooms or bathrooms. Health information in your plan is protected under HIPAA.

What we do not do

We do not sell devices, and we accept no fees or commissions from device makers, retailers, monitoring companies or installers. We do not provide 24-hour monitoring or emergency response. In an emergency, call 911.

Common questions

Do you monitor my parent?

No. We design the system and train your family. Alerts go to the people and services you choose. We do not provide 24-hour monitoring or emergency response.

Does it include a full home hazard check?

The deep dive focuses on technology. The full home hazard check comes from the core assessment it is added to.

Will it work during a hurricane?

Every plan includes a Storm and Outage Plan that shows what keeps working without power or internet and what the backup plan is.
